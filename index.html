Ovini Wijesooriya

Food Scientist, Emerging Researcher, Student Leader and Public Speaker from Sri Lanka.

Ovini is a final-year BSc (Hons) Food Science & Technology undergraduate at the University of Peradeniya and the Alfred Baur Gold Medalist (Most Outstanding Emerging Researcher) at FAuRS 2026. She also won Best Presenter and the Scientific Eye Photography competition, and was 1st Runner-up in Research Briefs (Sinhala).

Research and projects

Final-year research: an ADDIE-based mobile health platform to improve dietary behaviour and cardiometabolic health among University of Peradeniya undergraduates. Project: MealBridge.lk, a real-time food redistribution platform that tracks environmental impact to reduce food waste in Sri Lanka.

Leadership and experience

Local Committee Vice President at AIESEC in Sri Lanka; Secretary of the Indeewari Sub-Committee (Agriculture Faculty Students' Union); Head of Journalism at Perabeats Media Society; In-Plant Trainee (Quality Assurance) at CIC Dairies. Presented at the 24th International Students' Summit in Bogor, Indonesia.

Education

BSc (Hons) Food Science & Technology, University of Peradeniya. Diploma in Information Technology, University of Colombo School of Computing.
