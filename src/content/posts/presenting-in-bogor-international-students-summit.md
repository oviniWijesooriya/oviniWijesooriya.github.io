In 2025 I presented at the **24th International Students' Summit**, organised by Tokyo University of Agriculture, Japan, and held in Bogor, Indonesia.

Presenting to an international student audience is different from presenting at home. Few people in the room share your context, so every term, example and slide has to stand on its own.

## What the summit gave me

- **Practice at saying it simply first.** If a first-time listener cannot follow the idea in a sentence, the details will not help.
- **A wider view of the field.** Students from other countries and universities were working on the same big questions in food, agriculture and sustainability.
- **A foundation for what came next.** The speaking experience I built through debating, including the 2022 AFELA and Genero championships, carried into this summit, and the summit carried into FAuRS 2026, where I won Best Presenter.
