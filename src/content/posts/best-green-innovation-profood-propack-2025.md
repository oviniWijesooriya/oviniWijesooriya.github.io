The **Profood Propack AgBiz Exhibition 2025** is organised by the Sri Lanka Food Processors Association (SLFPA). I was placed **2nd Runner-up in the Best Green Innovation** category.

## Why this one stands out

Most of my awards come from academic settings. This one came from an industry exhibition, where the audience is the food processing sector rather than examiners. Being recognised there showed me that ideas about reducing waste and making food systems more sustainable carry weight beyond the university.

It also connects to what I am building: [MealBridge.lk](/blog/building-mealbridge-lk/), a platform that redistributes surplus food and tracks its environmental impact.

Photos from the exhibition are in [the Profood Propack gallery](/gallery/profood-propack-2025/).
