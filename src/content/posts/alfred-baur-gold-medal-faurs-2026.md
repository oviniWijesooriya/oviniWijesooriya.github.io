The Faculty of Agriculture Undergraduate Research Symposium (FAuRS) 2026 at the University of Peradeniya was the biggest moment of my degree so far. I came away with four awards, and the most important one is the **Alfred Baur Gold Medal for the Most Outstanding Emerging Researcher**, sponsored by Baurs Advisory.

## What I won

- **Alfred Baur Gold Medal**: Most Outstanding Emerging Researcher.
- **Best Presenter**: in the theme *Technological Interventions and Applications in Agriculture*.
- **Winner, Scientific Eye Photography**: the photograph is featured on the cover of the *Hanthana Blossoms* magazine.
- **1st Runner-up, Research Briefs (Sinhala)**: the brief was selected for publication in *Hanthana Blossoms*.

My department also featured me in Research Highlights as Best Presenter of the Department of Food Science & Technology, and nominated me for the Scientific Merit Award.

## The research behind it

The work I presented is my final-year research: the development and evaluation of an ADDIE-based mobile health platform to improve dietary behaviour and cardiometabolic health among undergraduates of the University of Peradeniya. You can read how that project works in [the research write-up](/blog/addie-mobile-health-research/).

## Why it matters to me

Two of these awards came from outside the usual research-paper format: a photograph and a brief written in Sinhala. Getting recognised for communicating research as well as doing it is something I want to keep building on.
