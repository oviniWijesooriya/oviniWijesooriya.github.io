My final-year research is titled *Development and Evaluation of an ADDIE-Based Mobile Health Platform for Improving Dietary Behaviour and Cardiometabolic Health among Undergraduate Students of the University of Peradeniya*. It won the Alfred Baur Gold Medal at [FAuRS 2026](/blog/alfred-baur-gold-medal-faurs-2026/).

## What ADDIE means

ADDIE is an instructional-design framework with five phases:

1. **Analysis**: understand the users and the problem.
2. **Design**: plan the content and the experience.
3. **Development**: build it.
4. **Implementation**: put it in front of real users.
5. **Evaluation**: measure whether it worked.

Building on a framework like this means the app is designed around evidence and evaluated properly, instead of being a collection of features.

## What the platform offers

- Personalised recommendations
- Dietary tracking
- Validated health assessment tools

Together these are meant to help undergraduates make healthier food choices and support their cardiometabolic health.
