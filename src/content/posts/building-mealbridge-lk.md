**[MealBridge.lk](https://mealbridge.lk)** is a live, real-time food redistribution platform I built to help reduce food waste and support food security in Sri Lanka.

## What the platform does

- **Redistributes food in real time**, so surplus food can reach the people who need it.
- **Tracks environmental impact**, so every redistribution shows what it saved, not just that it happened.

## Why a food scientist builds software

My degree is in Food Science & Technology, and I also hold a Diploma in Information Technology from the University of Colombo School of Computing. MealBridge sits where those two meet: food knowledge shapes what can be redistributed, and the IT side makes it practical to do quickly.

You can visit the platform at [mealbridge.lk](https://mealbridge.lk).
