Since 2024 I have held eight leadership roles at the University of Peradeniya and with AIESEC in Sri Lanka. Here is how they fit together.

## AIESEC

- **Team Leader, Customer Experience** (2024/25), AIESEC in University of Peradeniya
- **Team Leader, International Relations** (2024/25), AIESEC in University of Peradeniya
- **Event Manager, NOVA'25** (2025), AIESEC in University of Peradeniya
- **Local Committee Vice President** (2025/26), Outgoing Global Volunteer and B2B & B2C Marketing, AIESEC in Sri Lanka

## Campus societies

- **Secretary**, Society of Food Science & Technology (2024)
- **Director of Higher Education**, We Lead Career Skills Society (2024)
- **Head of Journalism**, Perabeats Media Society (2025)
- **Secretary**, Indeewari Sub-Committee, Agriculture Faculty Students' Union (2025/26)

## What the roles share

Each one is about getting a group of volunteers to deliver something together: an event, a publication, a programme. That takes planning, clear communication and follow-through, the same skills I use when presenting research or compering an event.
