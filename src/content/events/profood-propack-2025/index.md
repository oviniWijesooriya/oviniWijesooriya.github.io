The Profood Propack AgBiz Exhibition 2025, organised by the Sri Lanka Food Processors Association (SLFPA). I was placed 2nd Runner-up in the Best Green Innovation category.

The exhibition ran at BMICH, Colombo from 22 to 24 August 2025. I represented the Department of Food Science & Technology, whose stall showcased more than 25 novel products.
