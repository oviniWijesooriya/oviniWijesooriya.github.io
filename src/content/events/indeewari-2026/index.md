Indeewari is the annual musical concert organised by the Agriculture Faculty Students' Union to raise funds for the Needy Students' Scholarship Fund. The 2026 concert brought Bathiya & Santhush, Athma Liyanage, Ashanthi de Alwis, Raween Kanishka and Daddy to the stage, and raised LKR 4 million through donations, sponsorships and partnerships.
